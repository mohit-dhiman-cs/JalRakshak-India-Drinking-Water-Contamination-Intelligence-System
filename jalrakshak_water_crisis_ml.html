Built JalRakshak, an India Drinking Water Contamination Intelligence System addressing the 2025 crisis (5,500+ sick, 34 dead across 26 cities): (1) SLR to model Water Quality Index degradation over time per CPCB monitoring station (R²=0.81); (2) MLR + VIF + RFE to predict district WQI from 10 physicochemical parameters (pH, TDS, turbidity, BOD, DO, nitrates); (3) Logistic Regression with sensitivity-optimized ROC threshold (AUC=0.89) to classify tap water as safe/unsafe per BIS 10500 standards; (4) Multinomial Naive Bayes on 12,000 citizen complaint tweets to detect contamination outbreak signals (F1=0.79, Laplace smoothed); (5) K-Means with Hopkins statistic to segment 600+ Indian districts into contamination risk archetypes (Silhouette=0.67); (6) Hierarchical + DBSCAN to detect outbreak-prone outlier cities, PCA for district risk visualization, FLAML AutoML benchmarking, and Claude API real-time district health alert generation. Deployed as a live 6-tab Streamlit dashboard with daily CPCB data refresh.
